Our two Vienna apartments are for the same kind of trip, a city break, but they are different homes in different parts of town. One is in a calm residential quarter within walking distance of the old town, the other is a spacious duplex in a lively district further out. The choice mostly comes down to how many of you there are and how you plan to get around.

## Landstraße, 3rd district: the apartment near the old town

![Living room of the apartment in the 3rd district, with a sofa, a floor lamp and tall windows](/journal/vienna-landstrasse-or-ottakring-landstrasse.webp)

The [2BR Apartment Near Prater & City Center](/property/vienna-prater-two-bedroom) is in the Weißgerberviertel, a calm residential quarter within walking distance of Wien Mitte, the Danube Canal and the historic centre. Trams, buses and the U-Bahn are close by. St. Stephen's Cathedral, the State Opera, the Belvedere and Schönbrunn are easy to reach by public transport, and so is Vienna International Airport.

It has two bedrooms and one bathroom with a bathtub, and sleeps up to five. There is a desk and a laptop-friendly workspace, and the listing describes fast WiFi. It does not list parking.

## Ottakring, 16th district: the duplex

![Long dining table and the wooden staircase to the upper floor of the Ottakring duplex](/journal/vienna-landstrasse-or-ottakring-ottakring.webp)

The [Vienna City Duplex Apartment Ottakring](/property/vienna-ottakring-duplex) is a two-storey home in a lively district west of the centre, with good public transport into the city. It has four bedrooms and two bathrooms and sleeps up to nine, which makes it the one for a family or a group of friends who want to be under one roof.

There is a parking garage right next door where a space can be booked, and the property page shows the daily rate. A desk and a laptop-friendly workspace are listed here too.

## Which one for whom

- **Two to five guests, a few days of sights on foot or by tram:** the apartment in the 3rd district.
- **Six to nine guests, or a longer stay with room to spread out:** the Ottakring duplex.
- **Arriving by car:** Ottakring, with the garage next door. The 3rd-district apartment lists no parking.
- **A work trip:** both are set up for it, with a laptop-friendly workspace and good public transport connections.

## When to go

Vienna is a city for every season: museums, coffee houses and concerts do not depend on the weather.
